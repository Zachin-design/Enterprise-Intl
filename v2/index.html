Before You Ask

Questions buying committees ask

The questions that come up once a committee starts comparing training partners.

How is this different from self-paced video content or licenses we already have?

Licenses give people passive content, and completion rates tend to be low. Our programs have live faculty, projects on your own use cases, and skill growth you can measure against a baseline.

What if people fall behind?

A dedicated program manager tracks progress, sends weekly updates to leadership, and gives participants direct feedback, so problems surface while the program is still running.

Who owns what our teams build?

You do. Projects run on your use cases.

Can we run a limited proof-of-concept before committing at scale?

Yes. Start with one cohort, track, or function, review the first assessment data, and expand from there.

How long does a program run?

It depends on the format. Live Virtual runs 2 to 8 days, In Person runs 2 to 5 days, and Hybrid runs weekly over a couple of weeks. We set the schedule around your team’s bandwidth.

How do you keep up with industry trends?

We co-design curricula with practicing industry experts and update them based on ongoing research into new technologies.

Can you deliver at scale?

Yes. We have run upskilling programs for 10,000+ employees at Fortune 500 companies worldwide.
