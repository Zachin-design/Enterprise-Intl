Stage When What happens
  1. 01 Before we start

    Everyone takes a structured assessment. It shows where the group stands and identifies the people who are already ahead.

  2. 02 While it runs

    Hands-on projects double as ongoing assessment, so gaps show up early enough to act on.

  3. 03 On real data

    Projects use real data from real work scenarios, because conceptual learning on its own does not carry over to the job.

  4. 04 At the end

    A second assessment, built to compare directly with the first, shows exactly what moved.

  5. 05 60 and 90 days later

    We ask participants and their managers whether the training is being used on the job, which is the most honest measure of ROI.

Our Philosophy

Why we built it this way

A short conversation on designing enterprise training around measured capability instead of completion.

Before You Ask

Questions your team will raise

The objections that come up once a buying committee starts to form a view.

How is this different from self-paced video content or licenses we already have?

Licenses give people passive content, and completion rates tend to be low. Our programs are built for your teams, with live faculty, projects on your own use cases, and skill growth you can measure.

What if people fall behind?

A dedicated program manager tracks progress, sends weekly updates to leadership, and gives participants direct feedback so they come away with skills they can use in daily work.

Who owns what our teams build?

You do. Projects run on your use cases.

Can we run a limited proof-of-concept before committing at scale?

Yes. Start with a single cohort, layer, or function, and expand after reviewing initial assessment data.

How long does a program run?

It depends on the format and your team’s bandwidth. Live Virtual runs 2 to 8 days, Hybrid runs weekly over a couple of weeks, and In Person runs 2 to 5 days.

How do you keep up with industry trends?

We co-design curricula with practicing industry experts and update them based on ongoing research into new technologies.

Can you deliver at scale?

Yes. We have run upskilling programs for 10,000+ employees at Fortune 500 companies worldwide.
